Managed IT Services

Proactive monitoring, maintenance, cybersecurity, Microsoft 365 administration, backups, employee support, and onsite assistance for a predictable monthly cost.

Managed IT Services

GraNet provides ongoing technology support for small businesses that need dependable help without hiring a full internal IT department.

  • Proactive Monitoring: Keep an eye on computers, servers, backups, and critical systems before small issues become expensive problems.
  • Maintenance and Updates: Patch operating systems, applications, endpoint protection, and business software on a consistent schedule.
  • Cybersecurity: Help protect users, devices, accounts, email, firewalls, and sensitive business data.
  • Microsoft 365 Administration: Manage users, mailboxes, licensing, security settings, shared files, and account access.
  • Backup Planning: Make sure important company files are backed up, recoverable, and not trapped in one place.
  • Employee Support: Remote and onsite help for day-to-day IT issues, onboarding, access requests, and troubleshooting.
  • Network and Device Support: Support workstations, laptops, servers, Wi-Fi, firewalls, printers, phones, and related business technology.

Best for small businesses that want one clear IT contact, predictable support, stronger security, and fewer surprise technology emergencies.

Websites and Hosting

Your website should be easy to update, reliable, secure, and connected to the rest of your business technology.

  • Website Design: Build professional websites that clearly present your services, contact information, reviews, and service area.
  • Hosting: Set up and maintain dependable hosting for small-business websites.
  • Maintenance: Keep content, forms, security settings, domains, DNS, and site software in working order.
  • Support: Help with edits, updates, troubleshooting, contact forms, maps, analytics, and business profile links.
  • Technology Alignment: Connect the website to email, phone, business listings, security needs, and other operational tools.

Best for local businesses that need a professional website backed by the same team that can support the surrounding technology.

Repair and Project Work

Computer Repair and IT Projects

Not every problem needs a monthly plan. GraNet handles one-time issues, repairs, upgrades, and practical technology projects for businesses that need focused help.

  • Computer Repair: Troubleshoot slow systems, hardware issues, software problems, failed updates, and unreliable devices.
  • Remote and Onsite Support: Handle problems where they happen, whether that means remote help or an onsite visit.
  • Upgrades: Improve computers, storage, memory, operating systems, applications, and surrounding equipment.
  • Migrations: Move files, email, users, devices, domains, and business tools with less disruption.
  • Networking: Fix Wi-Fi problems, router issues, cabling questions, dead spots, device connections, and small-office networks.
  • Device Deployments: Set up new computers, laptops, phones, printers, software, accounts, and user access.
  • One-Time Projects: Tackle cleanup, vendor transitions, DNS work, website updates, security improvements, and general IT projects.

Best for urgent fixes, one-off jobs, smaller businesses, and projects that need skilled IT help without a recurring service plan.

Buying Guidance

Technology Purchasing and Consulting

Practical guidance on computers, servers, networking equipment, software, phones, and other business technology—so you buy the right equipment the first time.

Technology Purchasing and Consulting

Buying technology for a business is easier when someone can translate requirements, quotes, specs, and vendor recommendations into plain-English decisions.

  • Computer and Server Guidance: Choose systems that fit your workload, budget, growth plans, and support needs.
  • Networking Equipment: Evaluate routers, switches, wireless access points, firewalls, cabling, and related hardware.
  • Software and Licensing: Review business applications, Microsoft 365 plans, subscriptions, and licensing requirements.
  • Phones and Devices: Plan purchases for phones, laptops, desktops, tablets, printers, and accessories.
  • Quote Review: Review vendor quotes and explain what is useful, what is missing, and what may be unnecessary.
  • Procurement Planning: Help prioritize purchases so upgrades happen in the right order and within a realistic budget.

Best for businesses planning upgrades, comparing quotes, replacing aging equipment, or trying to avoid buying the wrong technology.
