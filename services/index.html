Free Consultation

An always free service that generates value before any fees or payment.

Always Free Consultation

  • Purchasing Advice: If you should upgrade, if you should downgrade, and how to maximize what you already have. Also covers what to purchase to achieve your intended goals, and how that purchase will generate value for your company.
  • After Action Review: If you had work done, recently purchased something, or have a quote for services, we can advise you on the value generated from that service.

Mobile Device Management

Laptops, desktops, tablets, and phones all need to work together. We help organize, secure, and support the devices your home or business depends on every day.

Mobile Device Management (MDM)

Your fleet of devices should not feel like a tangled cobweb.

  • Device Procurement & Setup: Procuring new laptops, tablets, and phones, then setting up those devices for your specific needs.
  • User Management: Setting up users, employee devices, email, device logins, access, and permissions.
  • Security Setup and Maintenance: Passwords, device locks, updates, antivirus, account recovery, and safer systems access.
  • Remote Work: Help connecting people to the files, email, apps, and systems they need.
  • Cleanup and Organization: Remove unnecessary clutter, old accounts, duplicate tools, and confusing setups.

Best for small offices, growing teams, families with multiple devices, or anyone whose tech setup has become hard to manage.

Business IT Solutions

Managed IT support for small businesses that need dependable help without hiring a full internal IT department.

Business IT Solutions / MSP Services

For businesses that need IT handled consistently, GraNet IT provides ongoing support for the systems you rely on: computers, servers, networks, firewalls, email, backups, websites, phones, and more.

  • Ongoing Support: Remote and on-site help for day-to-day IT issues.
  • Network Management: Routers, switches, firewalls, Wi-Fi, cabling, and troubleshooting.
  • Server and Workstation Support: Help maintaining business computers, shared systems, and local infrastructure.
  • Email and Cloud Support: Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, mail hosting, shared files, and account management.
  • Backup Planning: Help making sure important files are copied, recoverable, and not trapped in one place.
  • Vendor Guidance: We help you understand what you actually need before you spend money on tools, hardware, or software.
  • Data Backup and Migration: Secure your confidential data, such as customer data, photos, and sensitive company information.

Best for small businesses that want one clear IT contact, predictable support, and fewer surprise tech emergencies.

Hourly Services

Not every problem needs a monthly plan. For smaller jobs, one-time fixes, and cost-sensitive projects, hourly service keeps things simple.

Hourly IT Services

If you need help with a specific issue, project, setup, repair, migration, or upgrade, GraNet IT can work hourly instead of forcing you into a monthly service plan.

  • Computer Repair: Troubleshooting slow systems, hardware issues, software problems, and failed updates.
  • Setup Jobs: New computers, printers, email accounts, Wi-Fi equipment, phones, and software installs.
  • Data Help: File transfers, backups, migrations, cloud storage cleanup, and recovery planning.
  • Network Fixes: Wi-Fi problems, dead spots, router issues, cabling questions, and device connection problems.
  • Small Projects: Website updates, DNS help, email setup, vendor transitions, and general IT cleanup.
  • Advice Before Buying: We can help you avoid wasting money on the wrong equipment or service.

Best for residential customers, very small businesses, one-off projects, urgent fixes, or anyone who needs help without a monthly commitment.
