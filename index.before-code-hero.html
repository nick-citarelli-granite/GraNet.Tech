Security Designed for Small and Mid-Sized Organizations

Cybersecurity is not one product. GraNet combines practical safeguards, managed protection, recoverable backups, and clear operational procedures to reduce the likelihood and impact of ransomware, account compromise, hardware failure, and accidental data loss.

  • Ransomware Protection: Use layered endpoint security, monitoring, patching, access controls, and backup practices to reduce ransomware risk.
  • Endpoint and Account Security: Protect computers, users, email, credentials, and administrative access.
  • Business Data Backup: Back up critical company information so it is not dependent on one device, server, or cloud account.
  • Recovery Planning: Define how systems and information will be restored after an outage, attack, failure, or accidental deletion.
  • Email and Identity Security: Strengthen sign-in policies, multifactor authentication, permissions, and employee access.
  • Security Reviews: Identify obvious weaknesses, unsupported systems, missing controls, and unnecessary exposure.
  • CMMC IT Support: Help businesses understand, implement, and maintain technology safeguards aligned with their CMMC and NIST responsibilities.

Best for organizations that handle sensitive information, depend on continued access to data, or need a more structured approach to cybersecurity and recovery.
